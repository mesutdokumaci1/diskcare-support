Privacy Policy

Draft – requires legal review before publication.

Your privacy matters. All the health information you enter in DiskCare (pain entries, exercises, medications, appointments) is stored only on this device. No account is needed, and nothing is sent to a server or iCloud. Apple Health data is read only inside the app to show your progress; it is never used for advertising or marketing and is never shared with third parties. When you share a PDF report or export file, you decide what to share and with whom. Information under Turkish KVKK and the EU GDPR is available on our website. This text is a draft and will be legally reviewed before release.

GDPR

Health data is special-category data. DiskCare processes it only on your device, based on your explicit consent, for tracking your recovery. You can export or delete it at any time in Settings.
