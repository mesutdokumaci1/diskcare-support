DiskCare is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, does not recommend treatment and does not replace a doctor’s examination. The exercises and information in the app are general guidance; talk to your doctor or physiotherapist before using them. If your pain increases, spreads down your leg, or you notice numbness, weakness or problems controlling your bladder or bowels, stop exercising and seek medical help right away.
